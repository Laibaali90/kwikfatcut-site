The Roots

Three continents.One cuisine.

Jamaican cuisine is among the most diverse in the world — and that diversity is the direct result of history. The Taino people contributed cassava, sweet potato, corn, and the barbecue cooking method. West African enslaved people brought okra, callaloo, black-eyed peas, and an extraordinary knowledge of plant-based nutrition. Colonial contact introduced Asian and European cooking techniques and spices. The result is a cuisine of extraordinary nutritional depth — plant-forward, spice-rich, whole-food centred — that modern nutrition science continues to validate.

Signature Dishes and Their Nutrition

What Jamaican food actuallydoes for the body.

Ackee and saltfish provides complete protein, healthy fats from ackee, and iodine from the saltfish. Brown stew chicken with ground provisions delivers iron, complex carbohydrates, and the anti-inflammatory benefits of thyme, garlic, and scotch bonnet. Rice and peas — gungo or red peas with coconut milk — provides complete amino acids through the grain-legume combination, and lauric acid from the coconut milk. Callaloo soup delivers iron, folate, vitamins A, C, and K. Jamaican food was never the problem. The problem is how far we have drifted from it.
