Are environmental factors quietly changing the quality of our food?

Pollution, soil erosion, and climate change are affecting what nutrients make it onto our plates — often before anyone notices at the table.

"

Is economic pressure shaping what ends up on our shelves?

Systems that favour cheap, processed foods spread additives, preservatives, and artificial ingredients at scale — often prioritising profit over nourishment.

"

Why are so many people eating more — and getting less nutrition?

Modern diets have shifted heavily toward processed, refined, and convenience foods. High in calories, often low in the vitamins, minerals, and fibre our bodies depend on.

"

What happened to the traditional wisdom of how we used to eat?

Many traditional and indigenous diets were built around whole foods, healthy fats, fibre-rich vegetables, and legumes — with no refined sugar, no heavily processed ingredients.
