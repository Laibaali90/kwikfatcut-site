Born in Kingston —
Parish of St. Andrew.

I am Wilbert Blackwood — better known as Honey Ras, and online as Man Rasta. I was born and raised in Kingston, Jamaica, specifically in the parish of St. Andrew on the southeastern coast.

This region spans approximately 13 square miles and is renowned for its pristine white-sand beaches, crystal-clear waters, a vibrant cultural scene centred on physical sports, top-tier athletes, reggae and dancehall pioneers, and a warm, sunny climate ideal for outdoor activities.

Kingston — A city built from resilience

Kingston was founded in July 1692 as a refuge for survivors of the collapse of Port Royal harbour. A powerful earthquake caused tremors and land subsidence, with part of the harbour being submerged beneath the sea. From that disaster, a city rose.

Kingston quickly developed into Jamaica’s main city — its growth accelerated by seismic destruction. It surpassed Spanish Town (founded 1534, largely destroyed by fire in 1703) as the island’s capital and largest city. Today, Kingston is the vital political, economic, and cultural hub of Jamaica — a city that has always known how to rebuild.

Kwik Fat Cut —
the evolution.

On January 1, 2014, I dedicated myself fully to this mission. What began as 4Fat Loss Diet in Jamaica has grown into Kwik Fat Cut — a family health community focused on education, understanding, and sustainable health built without pressure, body shaming, or quick-fix thinking.

Focusing on indigenous health education first. Because good food is meant for eating. And every person deserves to understand the food they eat — where it comes from, what it does, why it matters.
