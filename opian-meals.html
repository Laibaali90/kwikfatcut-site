Jamaican Bitter Orange
Also known as Swible Orange in Jamaica — source of hydroxycitric acid (HCA)

Hydroxycitric acid (HCA) derived from Jamaican bitter orange inhibits citrate lyase — the enzyme responsible for converting excess carbohydrates into stored fat. By blocking this conversion pathway, HCA helps reduce fat accumulation at the metabolic level, not just through calorie restriction.

Clinical significance: HCA has been studied across multiple trials for its role in weight management. Its mechanism is distinct from stimulant-based fat burners — it works at the enzymatic level without elevating heart rate or creating dependency.

🍵
Chainyroot Tea Extract
Rich in catechins including EGCG — epigallocatechin gallate

Chainyroot tea extract is rich in catechins, particularly epigallocatechin gallate (EGCG) — one of the most studied natural thermogenic compounds known to science. It boosts thermogenesis: the process by which the body generates heat, leading to a sustained increase in metabolism and calorie burn even at rest.

Clinical significance: EGCG has been shown in multiple peer-reviewed studies to increase fat oxidation, support metabolic rate, and provide antioxidant protection against cellular damage. It works synergistically with the body's natural fat-burning processes.

🍎
Apple Cider Vinegar
High in acetic acid — appetite regulation and digestive support

Apple cider vinegar, high in acetic acid, works on two fronts: appetite suppression and digestive enhancement. It regulates hunger hormones including ghrelin, reducing caloric intake naturally. Simultaneously, it improves digestion by stimulating gastric motility and enzyme activity — ensuring nutrients are absorbed and waste eliminated efficiently.

Clinical significance: Studies have demonstrated acetic acid's role in improving insulin sensitivity, reducing post-meal blood glucose spikes, and supporting healthy gut pH — all of which contribute to more effective weight management.

These ingredients have undergone clinical testing to confirm their safety and effectiveness across all age groups, including children, adults, and seniors. Each component supports skin health by addressing hydration, elasticity, and barrier function tailored to each demographic.

Cooking Oils

What you cook
with matters.

Switching to healthier cooking oils provides nutritional benefits that extend well beyond the cooking process itself.

Cold-pressed coconut oil contains medium-chain triglycerides (MCTs), which boost metabolism, support weight management, and promote healthier skin and hair due to antioxidants.

Extra-virgin olive oil, rich in monounsaturated fats and polyphenols, helps reduce inflammation and lower cardiovascular risk.

Avocado oil, high in monounsaturated fats, vitamin E, and lutein, supports skin, eye, and heart health simultaneously.

Coconut water supports heart health, helps regulate blood sugar, and maintains proper hydration — rich in vitamin C, potassium, and electrolytes essential for water balance and muscle function.
