The Concept

What is an
Option Meal?

An Option Meal is not a fixed recipe. It is not a meal plan you subscribe to or a product you purchase. It is a conscious choice — one made with full understanding of what food does to the body, rooted in two of the most nutritionally coherent traditions ever documented: Rastafarian Ital eating and strict plant-based nutrition science.

The word “option” is deliberate. You have options. Not restrictions. Not guilt. Not pressure. When you understand what each food does, every meal becomes a choice you make knowingly — for your body, your energy, your clarity, and your longevity.

“An Option Meal begins where understanding begins. When you know what food does, choosing becomes natural. That is the Kwik Fat Cut way.”

The Rastafarian Foundation

Ital —
vital by nature.

The word Ital derives from “vital” — life force, energy, the animating principle of living things. In Rastafarian tradition, Ital eating is not a diet trend. It is a spiritual and physical commitment to consuming food that carries life force rather than diminishing it.

The core Ital principles have been practised in Jamaica and the Caribbean for generations. Long before nutritional science had language for what they were doing, Ital practitioners were eating in ways that modern research now confirms as among the most health-protective patterns on earth.

🌱

Natural and unprocessed

Food should come from the earth in its closest-to-original form. No artificial additives, no chemical preservatives, no synthetic flavourings. If it was not grown, it is not Ital.

🧂

Minimal or no salt

Traditional Ital uses sea salt sparingly or natural herbs for flavour. Excess sodium is understood as a burden on the kidneys and blood pressure — a position now supported by extensive cardiovascular research.

🍖

No flesh foods for strict practitioners

Many Ital practitioners avoid all meat. Others include fish. The underlying principle: the body is a temple, and what enters it should honour that. This is not judgement of those who eat differently. It is a personal covenant.

🏺

No synthetic containers

Traditional Ital avoids cooking in aluminium or synthetic materials. Clay pots, cast iron, and natural vessels are preferred. The vessel matters as much as the contents — a principle now echoed in growing research on microplastic contamination.

🌍

Connection to the land

Ital eating is inherently local and seasonal. What grows here, grows for us. The Jamaican earth provides callaloo, ackee, breadfruit, yam, sweet potato, guinep, soursop — each adapted to the bodies that live alongside them.

The Science

What strict plant-based
eating confirms.

Across the largest and longest nutritional studies ever conducted — including the Adventist Health Studies, the EPIC-Oxford study, and the China Study — strict plant-based diets consistently show lower rates of heart disease, type 2 diabetes, certain cancers, hypertension, and obesity.

This is not ideology. This is data from hundreds of thousands of people tracked over decades. The body responds to plant-based whole foods in ways that animal products and processed foods cannot replicate. Here is why:

One important note for strict plant-based eaters: Vitamin B12 is not reliably found in plant foods. Those avoiding all animal products should supplement B12 or include B12-fortified foods. This is not a weakness of the diet — it is simply biology, and the honest thing to say.

Where They Meet

Ital tradition &
plant science —
one convergence.

What is extraordinary about Ital eating is that its intuitions — developed through spiritual practice and generational observation in Jamaica — map almost perfectly onto what decades of nutritional science have now confirmed. Two paths. One destination.

The Ital practitioner avoids processed food because the spirit does not thrive on dead things. The nutritional scientist avoids processed food because it carries no phytochemicals, depletes gut bacteria, and drives inflammation. Different language. Identical conclusion.

The Option Meal is where these two traditions are gathered together. It draws from Ital’s spiritual clarity and its deep connection to Jamaican and West African food heritage. And it draws from the science of strict plant-based eating — the clinical evidence for what whole plant foods do inside the body at the cellular level. Together, they form something more powerful than either alone.

Practical Examples

Option meals
in practice.

These are not prescriptions. They are illustrations — showing how Ital tradition and plant science combine naturally in a single meal. Each one is built around whole, indigenous, largely Jamaican ingredients.

Morning — Ital Roots
Callaloo & Provision Breakfast
  • Steamed callaloo with onion, thyme & scotch bonnet
  • Roasted breadfruit or boiled yam
  • Sliced avocado (pear)
  • Fresh lime & sea salt (minimal)
  • Fever grass (lemongrass) tea

Iron, vitamins A & C, complex carbohydrates, healthy fats, digestive support. Sustained energy without blood sugar spike.

Midday — Plant Science
Legume & Grain Power Bowl
  • Slow-cooked red peas or gungo peas
  • Brown rice or wild rice
  • Steamed kale or pak choi
  • Pumpkin seeds & fresh herbs
  • Turmeric and ginger dressing

Complete plant protein, prebiotic fibre, anti-inflammatory compounds, magnesium, zinc. Full amino acid profile from peas and rice together.

Evening — Ital Stew
Jamaican Vegetable Rundown
  • Coconut milk base (natural, unsweetened)
  • Sweet potato, dasheen & cho cho
  • Green banana & plantain
  • Fresh thyme, allspice & scotch bonnet
  • Cerasee tea after the meal

MCTs from coconut, complex carbohydrates, potassium, fibre, digestive enzymes. Cerasee supports blood sugar regulation post-meal.

Light Meal — Raw Option
Living Fruit & Seed Plate
  • Mango, papaya & soursop
  • Guinep or star apple (seasonal)
  • Pumpkin seeds & shelled peanuts
  • Fresh coconut water to drink
  • Lime & a pinch of cayenne

Digestive enzymes from papaya & mango, vitamin C, natural electrolytes from coconut water, healthy fats and protein from seeds. Maximum living nutrition.

What the Body Gains

Not what you’re
giving up.

The conversation around plant-based eating is too often framed around what is removed — no meat, no dairy, no this, no that. That is the wrong frame entirely. The Option Meal conversation is about what you are adding. What you are giving the body that it has been waiting for.

When the body consistently receives whole plant foods it gains: more fibre than it can get from any other eating pattern. More phytochemicals. More antioxidants. More potassium. More magnesium. More folate. And it receives these compounds in the form they are most bioavailable — inside the whole food matrix, with all the co-factors nature designed to help absorb them.

This is why communities that have eaten this way for generations — Ital Rastafarians, Seventh-day Adventists, traditional Mediterranean communities, Okinawan elders — consistently show lower chronic disease rates and longer healthy lifespans. It is not the absence of meat that protects them. It is the presence of plant abundance.

Living It

Option Meals
as a daily practice.

An Option Meal does not require perfection. It does not require a complete dietary overhaul overnight. It requires one conscious choice at a time — guided by understanding rather than willpower, by knowledge rather than restriction.

Start with one Ital-inspired meal a day. Learn what it contains. Learn what it does. Let understanding do the work that discipline alone cannot. Over time, the choices become natural. The body responds. Energy changes. Clarity changes. The relationship with food changes from one of habit to one of intention.
